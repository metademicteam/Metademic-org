# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) — requested by the user. Intended future host: metademic.org.

## Users

Primary visitors are the general public plus researchers, builders, and institutions. The site is the public face of Metademic as an organization: someone arriving cold should understand what Metademic is, why it exists, and where to go next (open research, products, news, about).

Secondary audiences:

- Researchers and academics looking for papers, journals, and the scholarly platform at metademic.com
- Developers and labs who will later use RACN, a distributed LLM system
- Universities, labs, and partners evaluating Metademic as a research organization

## Product Purpose

Metademic is an academic AI lab and scholarly platform. It runs open research (journals, publications, and researcher infrastructure at metademic.com) and builds RACN, a distributed LLM system for science. This website is the organization homepage — the openai.com equivalent — not the scholarly information system itself.

Success: a first-time visitor can name Metademic, find Open Research (including metademic.com), find RACN under Products, and trust the organization enough to keep reading.

## Positioning

An academic-rooted AI lab that publishes in the open and is building a distributed LLM (RACN) for scientific work. Neighboring consumer AI companies cannot truthfully claim the scholarly platform at metademic.com as theirs.

## Operating Context

- Public marketing/org site, later hosted at metademic.org
- Open Research is a first-class nav destination and includes www.metademic.com (the existing scholarly information system: journals, publications, researcher profiles)
- RACN is a named product in the Products nav; the real product will be integrated later
- Visitors compare this site to openai.com in information architecture and craft

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed for this site:

- Organization homepage in the visual and IA language of openai.com
- Navbar includes **Open Research** and **Products**
- Products includes **RACN** (distributed LLM system)
- Open Research includes **www.metademic.com**
- RACN is navbar + a simple teaser page in this version; no live product integration yet
- No login, API console, or ChatGPT-equivalent product on this site yet

Undecided / not claimed:

- Pricing, customers, benchmarks, team roster, office locations, legal entity details
- RACN architecture specifics, availability, and API
- Careers listings

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Metademic** (wordmark, not a decorative logo lockup unless one is later supplied)
- Visual craft bar: **openai.com**, executed as a faithful category-standard clone — not a parody, not a “AI startup” remix
- Voice: serious research lab; plain, precise, non-hype
- Open Research must surface **www.metademic.com**
- Product name **RACN** (distributed LLM system) must appear under Products
- Future domain: metademic.org

## Evidence on Hand

Real, external:

- www.metademic.com — live scholarly information system (journals, articles, researcher profiles, news). Link to it; do not restyle or clone that product here.
- RACN — named by the user as “our distributed llm system product”; no public spec, screenshots, or benchmarks on hand.

Must not fabricate:

- Customer logos, testimonials, benchmark numbers, pricing, headcount, funding, or “used by” claims
- Fake papers presented as Metademic publications (illustrative research cards must be labeled as editorial/placeholder where a visitor could mistake them for real output)
- Photographs of a real team or offices we do not have

Authorable:

- Mission copy consistent with “academic AI lab + scholarly platform”
- Editorial art cards in OpenAI’s abstract/research-art style
- Placeholder news/research entries clearly in the site’s own voice, marked as sample where needed

## Product Principles

1. The organization site is a public research lab, not a consumer chatbot landing page.
2. Open research is as important as products; metademic.com is a real destination, not a footer link.
3. RACN is named and findable, but not oversold before it exists.
4. Craft matches openai.com; claims stay inside confirmed product truth.
5. Placeholders are honest. Invented commercial proof is forbidden.
